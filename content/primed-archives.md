The *Programa de Mejoramiento Integral de Barrios Subnormales de Medellín* (PRIMED) was a large-scale slum upgrading program implemented in Medellín, Colombia between 1993 and 2000. It intervened in 11 slums (out of 70) where approximately 25% of the informal urban population resided, focusing on infrastructure investments such as street pavement, pipeline and sewerage systems, and geological risk mitigation.
